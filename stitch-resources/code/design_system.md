## Brand & Style
The design system is engineered for a high-performance Agriculture Digital Platform, blending the precision of enterprise SaaS with the organic nature of the industry. The brand personality is **Intelligent, Grounded, and Efficient**. It moves away from "rustic" tropes in favor of a **Premium Minimalist** aesthetic, signaling technological sophistication and data-driven reliability.
